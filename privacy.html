Octopus Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

About Octopus

Octopus is a private file sharing app for closed groups. It is not offered to the public.

Google account access

Octopus requests access only to the Google Drive files that Octopus itself creates. It cannot see any other files, email, contacts or other data in a Google account. This access is used only to share files between members of the group.

How data is handled

  • Files are encrypted on the sender's device before they are stored anywhere.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Use of Google user data

Octopus's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device. Access to a Google account can be revoked at any time from that account's security settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the group's administrator.
